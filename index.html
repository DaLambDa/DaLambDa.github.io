About Da Fan

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Philosophy and Social Development at Shandong University. My research is in the philosophy of language and epistemology, with a focus on formal pragmatics, semantics, conditionals, and epistemic contextualism.

I received my Ph.D. in Philosophy from the University of California, Davis in 2021. Before joining Shandong University, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Wuhan University from 2022 to 2025. My other interests include logic, causation, and philosophy of mind.
